Core Technology

Built Different

Every XINFUJI home elevator is engineered around three pillars — structural efficiency, drive precision, and intelligent control.

Aluminum Alloy Shaft Frame

Industrial-grade aluminum alloy frame keeps the shaft footprint minimal while maintaining structural rigidity. Concealed joints and matching door panels deliver a seamless, built-in look.

  • Compact design — fits existing staircases
  • No load-bearing wall modification needed
  • Concealed wiring & piping channels
  • Powder-coated or anodised finish options

Steel Belt PMSM Gearless Traction

A permanent magnet synchronous motor paired with a flat steel belt drive delivers whisper-quiet operation, precise levelling, and energy efficiency far beyond traditional cable systems.

  • 100 mm flat belt — ultra-compact machine room
  • Energy class A — low power consumption
  • Near-silent operation (< 45 dB)
  • Imported precision bearings, long service life

Smart Control System Optional

A suite of intelligent features transforms the riding experience and gives homeowners complete control over access, comfort, and safety — all from a single panel or smartphone.

  • Facial recognition & fingerprint access
  • Voice command operation
  • Extended occupancy & door-hold alerts
  • Built-in air purification system
